## Core assignment prompt

This assignment asks you to examine the **prejudices, biases, and stereotypes** you hold about a particular **community or group**. After identifying where those biases, prejudices, or stereotypes may lie, you must **observe activity of that group or community** in an **online or in-person setting** and write a **personal reflection paper** that **critically examines** the experience.

Your paper must:
- focus on a specific **community or group**
- include an **observation** in an online or in-person setting
- critically examine your **own bias / prejudice / stereotype**
- be **5–7 pages**
- include **citations from class readings or digital sources**

Recommended: aim for at least **3–4** course readings cited (or more), unless the instructor prompt/syllabus specifies otherwise.

## Required thinking questions from the prompt

- How will you thoughtfully connect with this group?
- What do you need to consider for your own safety and well-being?
- What current myths or assumptions exist about this group?
- What does it mean to engage with this group when you have such assumptions?
- What does it mean to engage with this group in your current or future work setting?
- What assets or funds of knowledge did you observe?
- What can you learn from this group?
- What did you learn from this observation and reflection that will inform your future work?

## Recommended paper structure

1. **Introduction** — who/what observed, why chosen, clear research question
2. **Autobiographical section** — identity as raced, classed, gendered, abled person; bias clearly named
3. **Description of the research / observation** — who/what observed, how conducted, access/disclosure details
4. **Reflection / analysis** — examine biases, integrate course readings, theoretical orientation, consequences of bias, positionality
5. **Mechanics / presentation** — APA style, double-spaced, paginated, 5–7 pages, engaging title
