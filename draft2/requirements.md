## Core Prompt

Choose a community or group (based on your direct observations) about which you hold biases, prejudices, or stereotypes. Reflect on:

1. How you connect thoughtfully with this group
2. What myths, prejudices, and stereotypes exist about them and what their implications are
3. What assets and funds of knowledge you can identify in this group
4. What you have learned and plan to do differently in your future practice

---

## Suggested Sections

1. Introduction: Name your community and your prior assumptions
2. Connecting Thoughtfully: How do you/did you build relationship with this group?
3. Myths, Prejudices, Stereotypes: Name them, trace them, examine their impact
4. Assets and Funds of Knowledge: What strengths and resources does this group bring?
5. Future Practice: Concrete commitments for your classroom
6. Conclusion: How has your lens shifted?
