## Readings to Cite

### Annamma, Connor, & Ferri (2013) — DisCrit
- **Core idea:** Schools classify, manage, and sort students through overlapping race and disability lenses. Labels like "behavior problem" are not neutral — they reflect systemic patterns of who gets pathologized.
- **Where to use:** Section 3 (Myth vs. Reality) — connect the deficit framing of students to DisCrit's critique of how schools produce "problem" identities.
- **APA:** Annamma, S. A., Connor, D., & Ferri, B. (2013). Dis/ability critical race studies (DisCrit): Theorizing at the intersections of race and dis/ability. *Race Ethnicity and Education, 16*(1), 1–31.

### Talusan (2022) — Identity-Conscious Practice
- **Core idea:** Teachers must be conscious of how their own identities and assumptions shape their reading of students. Identity-conscious practice requires active self-examination.
- **Where to use:** Section 1 (Autobiographical grounding) and Section 5 (Future practice).
- **APA:** Talusan, L. (2022). *The identity-conscious educator: Building habits and skills for a more inclusive school.* Solution Tree Press.

### hooks (1994) — Teaching to Transgress
- **Core idea:** Education as the practice of freedom requires relational, humanizing pedagogy. Teachers must see and engage the whole student.
- **Where to use:** Section 4 (Assets) and Section 5 (Future practice).
- **APA:** hooks, b. (1994). *Teaching to transgress: Education as the practice of freedom.* Routledge.
