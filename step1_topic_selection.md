## Group/community being observed
The professional community of **teachers and instructional staff** at my elementary practicum placement.

## Specific setting
**In-person professional interactions** during my teaching placement, using **de-identified transcripts of everyday workplace exchanges**.

## Why this group/setting was chosen
I chose this group and setting because I am preparing to become part of this professional community, so examining my assumptions about it is personally and professionally important. I also already have a body of de-identified transcripts from routine interactions in my practicum placement, which gives me concrete observational material for reflection without needing to create a new observation site.

## Draft research question
How do my prior assumptions about teacher culture, belonging, and professional interaction in an elementary school setting shape the way I interpret everyday exchanges during my practicum placement, and how does analysis of de-identified transcripts complicate or revise those assumptions?

## Working anonymization note
In the paper, the site should be described as **"my elementary practicum placement"** or **"my placement school"** rather than by school name. People should be described using broad categories such as **teachers**, **instructional staff**, or **colleagues**, and all names or identifying details should be removed from transcript excerpts.

## Why this is a strong fit for the assignment
- It uses an already existing body of observational material.
- It keeps the setting directly tied to future professional practice.
- It allows the paper to remain anonymous while still being meaningful.
- It keeps the focus on reflection, interpretation, and deconstructing assumptions.
