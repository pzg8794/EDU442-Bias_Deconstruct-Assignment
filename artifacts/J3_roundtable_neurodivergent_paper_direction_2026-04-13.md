## How this guides the paper

- Sets the paper’s throughline: move from reading behavior as defiance to reading behavior as evidence of context, confusion, overwhelm, and unmet instructional needs.
- Clarifies the “observed group” is not a label (“troublemakers”) but students being positioned that way.
- Keeps the tone brilliance-centered and context-centered for neurodivergent + CLD learners.
