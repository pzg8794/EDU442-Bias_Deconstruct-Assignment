## Why this evidence matters (analytic memo)
This observation record captures the *same learner* being framed in two incompatible ways within one block:
- first, as a problem to control (public deficit talk), and
- later, as competent and socially valuable (invited into helping/leadership).

It also contains an explicit, asset-based statement that names cognitive difference as a strength—language that aligns directly with the paper’s commitment to center neurodivergent brilliance.
