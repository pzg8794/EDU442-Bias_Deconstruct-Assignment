## Why this evidence matters (analytic memo)
This lesson contains a clear contrast between:
- **public compliance/control talk** (treating voice/body as the “problem”), and
- **relational co-regulation** (offering responsibility, negotiated reinforcement, and competence narration).

That contrast is central to the paper’s argument that “problem student” is not a fixed trait, but an interactional category produced (and disrupted) through adult interpretation and classroom conditions.
