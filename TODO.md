**Chosen community: Teachers at Pine Brook Elementary School (Greece CSD)**

> ✔ Confirmed as of 2026-04-12. See rationale below.

**Why this is the right call:**

- You have **transcribed interactions** from every session at Pine Brook — this *is* your observation data. No reconstruction needed, no memory gaps.
- Teachers are a legitimate, academically defensible community for this assignment.
- Connects naturally to the course’s themes: teachers enact (or interrupt) bias in daily practice.
- Your positionality as a **student teacher who is also a minority** places you *inside* this community while also being subject to its biases — analytically rich and honest.

**What to mine from your transcripts:**

| What to look for | Why it matters for the paper |
|-----------------|------------------------------|
| How teachers talk about students of color or ELL students | Reveals deficit vs. asset framing |
| Who gets called on, praised, or corrected | Shows implicit bias in classroom practice |
| How teachers respond to your ideas or presence | Your experience as a minority student teacher is data |
| Assumptions made about families (language, engagement) | Connects to myths/stereotypes section |
| Moments of genuine care, creativity, or connection | Funds of knowledge / assets section |
| Things that surprised you or challenged your own assumptions | Your personal bias arc — the heart of the paper |
