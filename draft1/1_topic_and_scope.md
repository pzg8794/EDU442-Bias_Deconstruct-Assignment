## Observed group/community
Students in my elementary practicum classroom at my placement school.

## Specific setting
In-person classroom instruction and small-group support documented through my teaching-placement transcripts.

## Why this group and setting were chosen
This setting provides real observational material from my current work as a student teacher. It also connects directly to my future professional practice in education, which is central to the assignment.

## Working research question
How does observing students who might otherwise be labeled as difficult or off-task challenge the stereotype that behavior problems are fixed traits rather than responses to confusion, frustration, and insufficient support?
