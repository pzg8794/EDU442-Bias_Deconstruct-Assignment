## Bias being examined
The stereotype that certain students are inherently behavior problems, difficult, or unmotivated, rather than students who may be confused, overwhelmed, under-supported, or reacting to unclear expectations.

## Where this assumption may have come from
School settings often interpret visible frustration, noise, avoidance, or emotional escalation as defiance rather than as evidence of confusion, stress, or unmet instructional needs. Over time, those situational struggles harden into labels about who a student supposedly is.

## Why this matters
If I accept this stereotype too quickly, I risk misreading student behavior as character failure instead of communication. As a future educator, I need to ask what support is missing before deciding that the student is the problem.
